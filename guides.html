Making Tax Digital for Income Tax

Making Tax Digital (MTD) for Income Tax changes how sole traders and landlords report to HMRC. Instead of one tax return a year, you keep digital records in compatible software and send a short update every quarter, then a final declaration after the year ends.

Who has to join, and when

It depends on your qualifying income: your total gross income from self-employment and property, before expenses.

Quarterly deadlines

Updates cover the quarters to 30 June, 30 September, 31 December and 31 March, and are due by 7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May. The final declaration is due by 31 January, as now.

How we help

We set you up in MTD-compatible software, keep the records with you, and file each quarterly update and the final declaration for you.

Stamp Duty Land Tax from 1 April 2025

Temporary Stamp Duty reliefs ended on 31 March 2025. For residential purchases in England and Northern Ireland, the nil-rate band went back down from £250,000 to £125,000.

  • First-time buyers pay nothing up to £300,000 and 5% on £300,001–£500,000. Above £500,000 the relief doesn’t apply.
  • Additional properties (buy-to-let, second homes) pay a further 5% on every band.
  • Non-UK residents pay a further 2% on every band.
