Services

Everything HMRC and Companies House expect, handled

For sole traders, landlords, property investors and small limited companies. Pick one service or hand us the lot.

You & your propertyYour limited companyMonth to month
01

You & your property

Sole traders, landlords, investors and anyone HMRC has asked for a tax return.

  • Self Assessment tax returns

    We prepare and file your return, work out what you owe and your payments on account, and remind you well before 31 January.

  • Landlord & non-resident landlord tax

    Rental profit, allowable expenses and mortgage-interest relief, plus registration and returns under HMRC’s Non-Resident Landlord Scheme.

  • Capital Gains Tax

    Selling a property or shares. UK residential property sales must be reported and the tax paid within 60 days of completion. We calculate the gain, apply reliefs and file on time.

02

Your limited company

From the day you incorporate to every year-end after.

  • Company annual accounts

    Statutory accounts prepared and filed at Companies House, due nine months after your year end.

  • Corporation Tax returns

    Your CT600 return, with the tax worked out and planned for: payment is normally due nine months and one day after year end.

  • Confirmation statement

    Filed every year so the details Companies House holds on your company stay correct.

  • Company formation

    We set up your limited company and register it for Corporation Tax, and for PAYE and VAT when you need them.

03

Month to month

The regular work that keeps a business ready for its deadlines.

  • Bookkeeping

    Bank reconciliations, receipts and invoices kept up to date in Xero, FreeAgent or Hammock — monthly or quarterly.

  • VAT returns

    Registration once your turnover passes £90,000, and Making Tax Digital-compatible returns every quarter.

  • PAYE payroll

    Payslips, Real Time Information submissions to HMRC, workplace pensions and year-end P60s.

  • Financial reporting

    Management accounts and clear figures for you, your bank or your mortgage lender.

Questions

Good to know

Is the fee really fixed?

Yes. We agree the fee in writing before we start, based on what you need and your turnover. If your circumstances change — say you register for VAT — we’ll agree any change with you first.

Do I need to come to the office?

No. Meetings are by appointment, in person or online, and documents can be shared by email or through your cloud accounting software.

Can I switch to you from my current accountant?

Yes, at any point in the year. With your permission we contact your previous accountant for the records we need, so you don’t have to.

I live outside the UK but rent out a UK property. Can you help?

Yes. We handle the Non-Resident Landlord Scheme and the UK tax return that usually goes with it.
