Legal

Privacy & accessibility

How we handle your information, and how we make this site usable for everyone.

Privacy notice

IQTi Limited, trading as IQTi Accountants, of 128 City Road, London EC1V 2NX, is responsible for personal information you give us.

This website. The site does not use cookies, analytics or a contact database. The quote form runs in your browser and opens a message in WhatsApp or your email app; nothing is sent until you send it yourself.

What we use and why. When you contact us we use your name, contact details and the information you share to reply and prepare a quote. As clients, we hold the records we need to provide accounting and tax services and to meet our legal duties, including anti-money-laundering checks.

Sharing. We share information only with HMRC, Companies House and others where your instructions or the law require it, and with the software providers we use to do the work. We never sell personal data.

Keeping records. Client records are kept for as long as the law requires — normally six years after our work ends.

Your rights. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how we use it, by emailing info@IQTiAccountants.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this website. It is built to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA: it works with a keyboard and screen readers, text can be resized to 200%, colours meet contrast guidelines, and animation is switched off if your device asks for reduced motion.

Every page is available in English and Traditional Chinese. If something doesn’t work for you, email info@IQTiAccountants.co.uk or message us on WhatsApp and we’ll help — and fix it.

Tax guides and calculators

The guides and calculators on this site are general information, not advice for your situation. Rates and thresholds were correct when published and can change.
